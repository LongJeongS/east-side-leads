How do you generate the leads?+

From our own portfolio of local websites that already rank on Google for trade searches. We build them, we own them, and we've been ranking them for years. You get what they produce.

Do you build me a website?+

No, and that's the point. The websites are ours, already live and already ranking. There's nothing to build and no SEO project to wait on. You plug into the calls they produce, starting now.

Are the leads really exclusive?+

On a retainer, everything your site produces routes to you alone. On pay per lead, a live call is yours the moment you answer it, and we never resell a lead or pass it along afterward.

What counts as a qualified lead?+

A phone call that lasts longer than 30 seconds, or a form fill. Wrong numbers, spam, and robocalls don't bill. Every call is recorded, so you can hear exactly what you paid for.

What trades and areas do you cover?+

We run sites across home services and specialty trades, and we open new trades and areas regularly. The fastest way to find out is a quick call. If your area's taken, we'll tell you straight.

How fast do leads arrive?+

In real time. When a customer calls, your phone rings. When they fill out a form, it lands in your inbox the same minute.

Is there a contract?+

No. Pay-per-lead has no contract and no minimum. Retainers run month to month. If it's not working, you stop.

Can I refer someone?+

Please do. Reach out at seo@blakemitchell.ca for details on our referral program and revenue sharing.
